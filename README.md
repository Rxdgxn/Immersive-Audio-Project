# Audio based shooting range

This uses a mix of SteamAudio and FMOD in order to provide orientation in an enclosed space, purely based on sound (zero visuals).
A target produces a 3D sound when it spawns, as well as constant sonification through volume and pitch.
Higher pitch means the player should aim higher, lower for lower, and a stronger volume indicates a proper azimuth orientation.

Developed during the 2026 3DPUB summer school.
